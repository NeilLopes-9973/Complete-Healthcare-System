Blood Donation Facts

One donation can save up to three lives

Blood can be separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets to help multiple patients.

Every two seconds someone needs blood

The demand for blood is constant, making regular donations crucial.

You can donate every 56 days

Red blood cells need time to replenish, but you can donate plasma more frequently.

Type O-negative is the universal donor

People with O-negative blood can donate to anyone, making this blood type particularly valuable.
